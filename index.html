02 · The problem & research

Why does this matter?

Many schools cannot afford new devices or paid software. The team investigates how low-cost tools, open resources and reused hardware can make lessons clearer, more engaging and more inclusive.

Research question What can affordable technology change about how students learn? Expected evidence Reliable sources · short synthesis · source references
03 · School visit

Example School or Organisation

Students visit at least one school, library or education organisation, photograph the visit and explain how it uses technology today.

  • Which tools do teachers use right now?
  • What would they change if the budget allowed?
  • What does it cost, and who pays?
  • What happens when a device breaks?

07 · Educational impact

Small cost, big difference.

Four ways affordable technology can lift the quality of education.

Access

Reach students who have no device or connection at home.

Afford

Choose free, open and reused tools before buying new.

Engage

Make lessons interactive, visual and easier to remember.

Include

Support different languages, abilities and learning speeds.
