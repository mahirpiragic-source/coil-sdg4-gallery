What is SDG 4?

In 2015, all United Nations member states adopted the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, which contains 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). Goal 4 focuses on education. It aims to give every child, young person and adult the chance to learn, no matter where they were born, their gender, their family income or their abilities.

Why does education matter?

  • It helps people escape poverty and find decent work.
  • It improves health, for example through better knowledge about nutrition and hygiene.
  • It supports equality between women and men.
  • It builds peaceful, tolerant and democratic societies.
  • It prepares people to protect the environment and the climate.

What are the main challenges?

  • Poverty and the cost of schooling keep many children out of classrooms.
  • Conflict, displacement and natural disasters interrupt learning.
  • Many countries lack enough trained teachers.
  • Girls and children with disabilities face extra barriers.
  • Many students attend school but still do not learn basic reading and maths.

The 10 targets of SDG 4

Each target describes a specific result the world wants to achieve by 2030.

4.1

Free primary and secondary education

All girls and boys complete free, equitable and quality primary and secondary schooling.

4.2

Early childhood development

All children have access to quality early development, care and pre-primary education.

4.3

Technical and higher education

Equal access for all women and men to affordable technical, vocational and university education.

4.4

Skills for work

More young people and adults have the skills needed for jobs, decent work and entrepreneurship.

4.5

Equality in education

Eliminate gender disparities and ensure equal access for vulnerable groups, including people with disabilities.

4.6

Literacy and numeracy

All young people and a large share of adults can read, write and do basic maths.

4.7

Education for sustainable development

Learners gain the knowledge for sustainable lifestyles, human rights, gender equality and global citizenship.

4.a

Education facilities

Build and upgrade safe, inclusive and effective learning environments for all.

4.b

Scholarships

Expand scholarships for students from developing countries to study abroad.

4.c

Qualified teachers

Increase the supply of qualified teachers, especially in the least developed countries.

How can we help?

Quality education is not only the job of governments. Everyone can contribute, including students.

As a student

  • Help classmates who find a subject difficult.
  • Donate books, school supplies or devices you no longer use.
  • Take part in school projects and volunteering.
  • Respect and include everyone in your class.

As a community

  • Support organisations such as UNESCO, UNICEF and local charities.
  • Invest in libraries, free courses and adult learning.
  • Train and fairly pay teachers.
  • Make schools safe and accessible for everyone.
